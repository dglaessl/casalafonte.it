Welcome

A stylish farmhouse, all yours, in perfect quiet

Casa La Fonte has stood on the edge of San Dalmazio for about four hundred years. Between 2018 and 2020 Scandinavian architects restored it with a light touch: the old stone, beams and terracotta stayed, and everything added since is simple, modern and well made.

What our guests mention most is the calm, and the pool. The village has a few dozen houses and one family restaurant. From the terraces and the private heated saltwater pool you look south over vineyards, olive groves and wooded hills, and on most evenings the only sounds are swifts and the church bell.

We bought the house as our future home in Italy. Until then we share it with other families, and we answer every request ourselves.

Daniel & Eva

I

Design

Half-metre stone walls, chestnut beams and handmade terracotta floors are the backbone of the house. The architects left them visible and kept the rest calm: white walls, oak, linen, black steel and good lighting.

The furniture is a mix of Scandinavian classics and pieces found in Tuscany. Nothing is there for show; the long oak table, the reading chairs and the kitchen island are simply where everyone ends up.

II

Serenity

The whole property is yours. The main house, the guesthouse, the garden and the pool are hidden from the lane, and no one else stays on the grounds while you are there.

Mornings start with coffee on the terrace in the sun. Afternoons belong to the pool and the shade of the pergola. In the evening you can walk up to the village square for dinner, or stay in and watch the light fade over the hills.

The house

Room for up to seven

A main house and a separate guesthouse share the garden, the terraces and the pool. Up to seven guests sleep in three bedrooms, with two bathrooms.

Main house, ground floor

Kitchen and dining room

The heart of the house. A well-equipped kitchen with a large island opens into the dining room, where an oak table seats eight under the old beams. French doors lead straight out to the terrace, so breakfast often moves outside.

Main house, ground floor

The living room

One generous space that works for everyone: a reading corner by the wall of books, deep sofas for being together, and a built-in daybed by the TV that sleeps a seventh guest. A single sofa bed can go in whichever room you like. The thick walls keep it cool on summer afternoons.

Upstairs

Main House – Bedroom 1 & 2

Bedroom 1 has a king-size bed and a baby crib on request; Bedroom 2 has a queen-size bed. Both have beamed ceilings and views over the garden. Next door is the newly renovated bathroom.

Across the garden

Guesthouse – Bedroom

A former stone outbuilding, now a private suite with a queen-size bed, its own bathroom and the washing machine. Its terrace sits under a vine-covered pergola. Grandparents, a second couple or teenagers love the independence.

Outside

Terraces and loggia

Stone terraces on several levels follow the slope. There is a long table in the open, a covered loggia with a gas barbecue for warm evenings, sun loungers, and plenty of quiet corners to read in the shade.

Outside

Heated saltwater pool

A private saltwater pool, heated by a heat pump, with an outdoor shower and views south over the valley. Swim before breakfast, or at dusk when the hills turn gold.

Good to know

Facts about the house

Arrival

Check-in from 4 pm, check-out by 10 am. For your satnav, use Ristorante La Rocca on the village square; the house is just below it.

The lane

A short, steep lane of about 50 metres leads down from the square to the house. Any normal car manages it easily; with a van or a larger car, there is free parking on the square, a minute's walk away.

A car

The best way to explore the area. Pisa airport is about 1 h 20 away, Florence about 1 h 30, and the supermarket in Pomarance about 15 minutes.

Cooling

Half-metre stone walls keep the house naturally cool, and every bedroom has its own fan for the warmest nights. Open the windows in the evening (all have insect nets) and close them by day, and the house stays pleasant without air conditioning.

The landscape

San Dalmazio is close to Larderello, the birthplace of geothermal energy, the same clean energy that heats the house and its hot water.

Dogs

Very welcome, for €75 per stay. The garden is not fenced and there is a steep drop behind the lawn, so please keep an eye on them there.

Included

Wi-Fi, bed linen and towels, washing machine, baby crib, free parking.

Wellbeing

Move, rest, eat well

The hills around the house are made for an active, healthy holiday. Much of it starts at the door, and the rest is a short drive away.

Hiking from the house

Trails lead from the village up to the Rocca Sillana fortress and through the steaming white rocks of the Biancane.

Horse riding

Rides along quiet country trails at Fattoria Tegoni, 15 minutes away: 1½ hours from €52 per person, lessons from €32.

Thermal springs: our top three

Sasso Pisano, 30 minutes: free natural pools at about 36°C in a village of steaming springs.
La Cerreta at Sassetta, about 45 minutes: a calm spa on an organic farm, with pools from 34 to 38°C. Book ahead.
Calidario at Venturina Terme, about an hour: a warm spring-fed lake in a pine wood, easy to combine with a day at the sea.

A day at sea

Bolgheri, about an hour: drive the famous cypress avenue, taste the local wines, then swim at Marina di Castagneto.
Rimigliano near San Vincenzo, about an hour: a long, quiet beach backed by pine woods, close to Calidario.
Baratti, about 1¼ hours: a curved bay below Populonia, the only Etruscan town built on the sea. Paddle boards and boats to rent in summer; stay for the sunset.

Cooking: classes and chefs

At the house: Arianna & Friends make fresh pasta and a Tuscan meal with you in the big kitchen (a class from €480 for two), or cook dinner while you sit back (from €300 for two), plus a €120 travel fee. Always private, just for your group, and they cater for any diet. In English, German, French or Spanish. Book before you travel: they are often fully booked, and last-minute requests are hard to fit in.
A local cook and her mother can also come to make a traditional Tuscan dinner: mention it in your request.
At the farm: cook a Tuscan lunch with the family at Fattoria Tegoni, 15 minutes away, and then eat it: 2–3 hours for 2–6 people, €90 per adult, €70 per child.

Farm lunch and tastings

At Fattoria Tegoni: a set farm lunch for €35, or Cinta Senese cured meats and local cheese with a glass of wine for €25. Book directly with the farm by email or WhatsApp.

A welcome basket

Arrive to a basket of local food, put together by our team at Convivum and waiting at the house when you check in. Just mention it in your request.

Day tours with Arianna & Friends

The same team runs private tours around Tuscany, from Vespa rides through the hills to food and wine days. Tours that include a winery or another partner take about a day to confirm. See the options on their website.

From June to September 2027, Serena Calastri, an Iyengar yoga teacher from Volterra, can teach at the house in English (book at least 48 hours ahead). We are still lining up personal training and massage: mention it in your request and we'll tell you what we can arrange for your dates.

The area

Between Volterra and the sea

San Dalmazio lies in the Val di Cecina, one of the quieter corners of Tuscany. Few tour buses come this way: the roads wind through oak woods and wheat fields, and the villages still keep their own rhythm. Etruscan towns, wine estates and the coast are all within an hour. About us

Who we are, and how we got here

We are Daniel and Eva. We were both born in Germany and both trained as architects. Daniel has stayed with buildings; Eva moved into software development and has worked at Google since 2015.

In 2005 we left Germany for London, where we lived until 2012. Then came three years in Singapore, and in 2015 we moved to Silicon Valley. In between we travelled whenever we could, across Europe and especially through South East Asia.

These days we split our time between California, where Eva mostly is, and Texas, where Daniel mostly is. Casa La Fonte is where we come together. We fell for it as architects: the half-metre walls, the old beams and the light over the valley, kept honest by a careful restoration.

At home in San Jose we keep a small farm: our dog, six cats and eleven chickens, plus two horses who live at a stable. So when guests ask whether they can bring their dog to Casa La Fonte, the answer is yes!

One day it will be our home in Italy. Until then we share it with guests, and every request still comes to the two of us.

Questions and answers

How do I book directly?

Enter your dates in the form under Prices and send us the request. We reply within a day with a short rental agreement; a 30% deposit by card (secure Stripe link) or bank transfer confirms your booking.

What will our stay cost?

Enter your dates in the form and press “See the price”: the total appears straight away. Final cleaning, bed linen, towels and pool heating are included, and there are no service fees.

When can we come?

The house is open from 25 March to 31 October. Send us your dates and we’ll tell you within a day whether they’re free.

How many guests fit?

Up to seven: Main House – Bedroom 1 (king-size bed) and Bedroom 2 (queen-size bed), the Guesthouse – Bedroom (queen-size bed), a built-in daybed in the living room and a single sofa bed that can go in any room. A baby crib is available.

What is the minimum stay?

Four nights in spring, June, September and October. Seven nights in July and August.

Is the pool private and heated?

Yes. The saltwater pool is only for your group and is heated by a heat pump.

Is there air conditioning?

The house has no air conditioning, but it doesn't need much help: the thick stone walls keep it naturally cool, and every bedroom has a fan for the warmest nights.

Do we need a car?

Yes, it's the best way to explore. Pisa airport is about 1 h 20 away, Florence about 1 h 30. The lane down to the house is short and steep; with a larger car, park for free on the village square.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, up to two dogs are very welcome, for €75 per stay. Add them in the request form. The garden is not fenced, and there is a steep drop behind the lawn.

Can you arrange yoga, a trainer or hikes?

Hiking trails start at the village, and horse riding and thermal springs are close by. Arianna & Friends can give a cooking class or cook dinner at the house, and from June to September an Iyengar yoga teacher from Volterra can come to teach. For a personal trainer or massage, mention it in your request and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

What if we need to cancel?

If you cancel up to 60 days before arrival, we refund your deposit in full. After that, what you have paid is non-refundable, unless we can rebook your dates. We recommend travel insurance.
